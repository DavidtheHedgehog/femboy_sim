# 赛博男娘模拟器 :3 (Cyber Femboy Simulator)

![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)
![Language](https://img.shields.io/badge/Language-Bilingual%20(EN/ZH)-pink)

A deeply fried, over-the-top, absurdist text-based simulator about navigating the treacherous path between Linux elitism, crippling internet addiction, unhinged physical degradation, and cyber-spirituality. Will you maintain your masculinity and sanity, or will you descend into the terminal-brainrot abyss? 

一个极度精神污染的纯文本驱动的人生模拟器。你将游走在 Linux 狂热、互联网重度依赖、肉体改造、赛博精神病与禁忌快感的万丈深渊之中。你是能守住生而为人的最后底线，还是会在赛博魅魔的深渊中彻底堕落成烂泥？

## Features / 核心特性

- **Bilingual Support (双语支持):** Enjoy the brainrot in both English and Chinese seamlessly at the click of a button! 
  （原汁原味的抽象中文与发狂英文双重体验）。
- **7 Dynamic Physiological & Psychological Stats (七大动态生理属性):**
  - Cuteness (浓度 / :3)
  - Taboo Pleasure (禁忌快感阈值)
  - Monster Energy (魔爪重度污染)
  - Arch Linux Skill (终端浸透/内核掌控力)
  - Masculinity Left (原生阳刚残留)
  - Family Disappointment (家族蒙羞指数)
  - Mental Stability (大脑理智崩盘率)
- **Extreme Random Events (极致混乱的随机事件):** From installing Arch Linux at 3 AM to forced dark-web multiparty routing. 
- **10 Pathological Endings (10 大病态终局):** Ascend as a Futa Demon, get shipped to a boot camp by your ex-military Dad, or melt your prefrontal cortex with extreme hardware stimulation.

## Technologies Used / 使用技术

- **React Element Foundation**
- **TypeScript** (Strict mode for disciplined chaos)
- **Tailwind CSS** (Pink/Neon palettes, scanline terminal aesthetics)
- **Framer Motion** (CRT shake effects, fluid layout transitions)
- **Lucide Icons**

## Survival Rules / 生存法则

> "You may abandon masculinity, you may sell your dignity, but do not let your sanity completely shatter before you find absolute truth in the matrix."
> “你可以放弃阳刚，你可以出卖尊严，但请在理智彻底崩塌前死守底线。”

Navigate the 12 events to try to survive. Your choices will permanently alter your stats. Watch your `Mental Stability` closely!
